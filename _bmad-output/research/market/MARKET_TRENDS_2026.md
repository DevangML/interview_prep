## 1. Macro Market Dynamics (2026 Status)

1. **Volume Compression vs AI Growth:** Overall traditional IT service openings contracted ~24% YoY, while dedicated AI/ML and AI-integrated software engineering roles expanded by +40-50%.
2. **The 1-3 YOE Sweet Spot:** 1-3 YOE candidates represent **31% of all active engineering job listings**—the largest single slice of market demand (compared to Freshers at 29%, 3-5 YOE at 15%).
3. **Average Time-to-Fill:** 23 days for 2-3 YOE roles. Employers move fast once candidates clear initial screening gates.

---

## 2. Key Hiring Bar Shifts in 2026

- **Shift 1: AI Tool Integration as JD Requirement (28% of JDs):** Employers now explicitly look for candidates who write code using AI tools, construct system prompts, and handle LLM integrations.
- **Shift 2: AI Video & Gamified Aptitude Screening:** Companies like KPIT, Capgemini, and TCS use automated AI platforms (AON, HireVue) as the first elimination gate before any human engineer sees the code.
- **Shift 3: Rapid Viva over Problem Solving at Service/Mid-Tier:** L1 rounds at LTIMindtree, Persistent, and Amdocs test rapid-fire retrieval (70% viva / 30% coding).
