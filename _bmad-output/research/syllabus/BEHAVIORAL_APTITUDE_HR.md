## 1. Overview & Tier Requirements

## 2. Topic Inventory & Core Modules

### A. STAR Framework & Project Story Scaffolding
- **STAR Architecture:**
  - **Situation (15%):** Provide high-level context, project scope, team size, and tech stack. (e.g., *"At my previous company, our Frappe-based warehousing backend was processing 50,000 orders/day during flash sales..."*)
  - **Task (15%):** Clearly articulate your specific ownership and the problem statement. (*"I was tasked with reducing database query latency on the checkout API from 1200ms to under 200ms..."*)
  - **Action (50%):** Detail the exact technical decisions, debugging steps, trade-offs, and implementation YOU personally executed. (*"I profiled the API with EXPLAIN ANALYZE, identified an N+1 query pattern on the DocType child table, refactored the ORM query with prefetch calls, and implemented a Redis Cache-Aside layer with 15-minute TTL..."*)
  - **Result (20%):** Deliver quantifiable business and technical metrics. (*"This reduced 95th percentile latency by 85% down to 180ms, handled 3x peak load without crashing, and saved $400/month in cloud DB scaling costs."*)

- **Required 4-Story Matrix for 2-3 YOE Engineers:**
  | Story Category | Focus Area | Core Trait Evaluated |
  |---|---|---|
  | **1. Complex Technical Challenge** | P0 Outage / Latency / Refactoring | Problem-solving depth & debugging rigor |
  | **2. Conflict Resolution** | Architectural disagreement with team | Communication, empathy & technical compromise |
  | **3. Overcoming Deadline Pressure** | Scope trade-offs under tight launch | Prioritization, ownership & pragmatic execution |
  | **4. Ownership & Leadership** | Mentoring junior / Introducing tool | Proactivity, initiative & team impact |

### B. HR Viva & Negotiation Strategies (India Market)
- **Elevator Pitch ("Tell me about yourself"):** 90-second structured script covering current role, primary tech stack (e.g., Python/Frappe/Flutter/SQL), major technical achievement, and reasons why the target position matches your career trajectory.
- **Why are you looking for a change?:** Frame positively around seeking technical growth, scale challenges, and expanded scope. NEVER badmouth current management or company environment.
- **Handling Weakness Questions:** Name a genuine technical area you actively improved (e.g., *"Earlier I relied mostly on default ORM queries, but after encountering a production bottleneck, I invested time into learning raw PostgreSQL query execution plans and index optimization."*).
- **CTC & Salary Negotiation Breakdown:**
  - Understand Indian CTC components: **Fixed Base Pay** vs **Variable / Performance Bonus** vs **Joining / Retention Bonus** vs **ESOPs / Stock Vests**.
  - **Notice Period Management:** Handling preferences for 30-day notice periods vs standard 90-day service company notice periods (using buyout options or early release negotiations).

### C. Quantitative Aptitude & Logical Reasoning
- **Quantitative Aptitude Core Topics:**
  - **Time & Work:** Man-hours formula ($W = M \times D \times H$), Pipe & Cistern problems.
  - **Speed, Distance & Time:** Relative speed, Trains crossing platforms/bridges, Boats & Streams (Upstream vs Downstream speed).
  - **Permutations & Combinations:** Seating arrangements, selection combinations ($^n C_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$).
  - **Probability:** Independent events, conditional probability, dice and card problems.
  - **Profit & Loss & Percentages:** Cost Price, Selling Price, Marked Price, Discount percentages, Effective percentage change formula ($A + B + \frac{AB}{100}$).
  - **Ratio & Proportion:** Mixture and alligation problems.
  - **Data Interpretation (DI):** Reading and calculating percentages/ratios from Bar graphs, Pie charts, and Data tables quickly under timed conditions.

- **Logical Reasoning Core Topics:**
  - **Coding-Decoding:** Alphabet position shifts, numerical encoding.
  - **Syllogisms:** Venn diagram representations ("All A are B, Some B are C").
  - **Blood Relations:** Family tree mapping.
  - **Seating Arrangement:** Linear rows and Circular tables (facing inward vs facing outward).
  - **Clocks & Calendars:** Angle between hour and minute hands ($\theta = |30H - 5.5M|$), finding day of the week for any date.

### D. AI Video Interview Strategies (AON / HireVue / Talview)
- **AI Scoring Engine Mechanics:**
  - **Speech Pacing & Clarity:** Aim for 120–150 words per minute. Avoid long awkward pauses or ultra-fast rushed speaking.
  - **Keyword Detection:** AI models scan audio transcriptions for specific technical keywords matching the job description (e.g., "PostgreSQL", "Redis", "STAR method", "Latency", "Microservices").
  - **Structural Compliance:** Automated checks for clear narrative structure (Beginning, Middle, End).
  - **Visual Engagement:** Maintain eye contact directly into the webcam lens (not down at your screen), maintain steady lighting, and minimize background movement.
- **Preparation Workflow for Asynchronous Video Prompts:**
  - Use the 30-second preparation timer to jot down 3 core bullet points on a physical scratchpad using the STAR framework.
  - Deliver responses cleanly within the 2-minute recording window without filler words ("um", "like", "actually").
