The question this answers
Who owns this allocation, and how do we know it is not used after it dies?
6 answers · 1 viewed

Mechanism

Affine types plus a borrow checker, with lifetime elision. Move by default; Copy is opt-in. Each value has exactly one owner and the compiler frees it when that owner leaves scope.

Why this way

The only route to memory safety without a runtime — which the decision to have no garbage collector made mandatory.
Price paid

Steep curve; fighting the checker; cycles need Rc<RefCell<>>; unsafe is the escape hatch.
