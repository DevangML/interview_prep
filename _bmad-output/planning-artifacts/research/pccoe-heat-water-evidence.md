# PCCOE hackathon: Heat Guardian and Water Sentinel evidence audit

Research date: 8 September 2026. Independent technical and market evidence for the two proposals in the supplied attachment. The event's exact rules and eligibility are outside this subreport; recommendations are conditional on an open climate/IoT problem track. Source facts, engineering inferences and proposed targets are distinguished. No buyer interviews, component purchases, field trials or measured model results have occurred.

## Decision

Both problems are useful. Neither original specification is ready to promise to judges. Water offers an excellent controlled physical experiment if the team already has a pipe rig and DSP skills. Heat becomes credible with a shared, validated environmental station and inexpensive alert badges; the proposed autonomous ₹1,000 personal instrument is a much harder measurement project than it appears. Novelty is in local deployment, verification and workflow, not invention of acoustic leak detection or wearable heat alerts.

## 1. Refined Heat Guardian: Worksite Heat Advisory and Acknowledgement Network

**Pitch:** A worksite station measures environmental heat exposure; offline badges deliver Marathi-backed vibration alerts and let workers acknowledge a supervisor's heat policy. The dashboard exposes sensor quality, task category, clothing/acclimatization assumptions, and missed acknowledgements. It supports preventive worksite decisions; it does not know an individual's dehydration, core temperature or exact safe endurance.

### Claims requiring correction

1. **The proposed sensor set is incomplete for the claimed measurement.** Conventional WBGT uses natural wet-bulb, globe and dry-bulb temperatures. Outdoors with solar loading, `WBGT = 0.7 Tnwb + 0.2 Tg + 0.1 Tdb`; without solar loading, `0.7 Tnwb + 0.3 Tg`. RH is not a direct replacement for natural wet-bulb. OSHA describes tested waterless instruments using temperature, RH, globe and airflow. Clothing, workload and acclimatization matter alongside the environment. [OSHA technical manual](https://www.osha.gov/otm/section-3-health-hazards/chapter-4).
2. **“Implement ISO 7243” is not equivalent to issuing personalized drink/stop prescriptions.** ISO describes a screening method across the working day, up to eight hours, and expressly excludes very short heat exposures. The public abstract does not provide implementation tables; acquire and review the actual standard before claiming conformity. Label any prototype rules with their named public source/version and assumptions; do not invent a 15/45 cycle under the ISO name. [ISO 7243:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/67188.html).
3. **The ping-pong globe is a prototype sensing experiment, not a ₹10 calibrated instrument.** A conventional globe is 150 mm. Nonstandard, smaller devices can differ from reference measurements. Material, diameter, emissivity, sensor placement and wind response need comparison and correction. [OSHA measurement discussion in proposed-rule economic analysis](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/Heat-NPRM-Final-Econ-to-Addtl-Requirements.pdf). Engineering inference: a body-worn globe also needs tests for body heat, clothes shading and orientation; stationary siting is a simpler MVP.
4. **An accelerometer measures movement, not total metabolic heat.** Engineering limitation: carrying a heavy static load, pushing, climbing and different tool use can have similar motion traces but different demands. Make task categories supervisor-selected first. Use accelerometry as an optional activity cue with an unknown state, not as sole authority to downgrade risk. NIOSH's prevention guidance calls for lighter workloads and longer breaks for new/unacclimatized workers, and adaptation to conditions. [NIOSH workplace recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/recommendations/index.html).
5. **A 20-minute “strain trajectory” is unsupported without labels and validation.** Forecasting an environmental threshold is distinct from predicting a worker's physiological strain. MVP: show measured trend and a clearly marked threshold-crossing estimate only under stable assumptions; no forecast should suppress present-time alerts. An original model architecture alone is not evidence of predictive value.
6. **Marathi alerts do not need an ASR model.** IndicConformer is speech-to-text. Use reviewed prerecorded Marathi messages for deterministic alerts, or separately selected TTS. ASR belongs in optional spoken input, with device/runtime testing. [AI4Bharat IndicConformer README](https://github.com/AI4Bharat/IndicConformerASR/blob/master/README.md).
7. **The 60-second heat-gun demonstration does not validate occupational WBGT.** Even Kestrel's commercial device specifies about eight minutes to 95% and fifteen minutes to 99% WBGT accuracy after large environmental changes. [Kestrel response specification](https://kestrelinstruments.com/kestrel-5400-heat-stress-tracker). A damp cloth over an RH sensor is not a natural wet-bulb installation and can cause condensation artifacts. Prefer a stabilized, monitored lamp/fan experiment plus explicitly labeled replay of a previously collected threshold crossing. Do not heat a badge while it is worn, a battery, or a plastic globe with a heat gun.
8. **Remove the unsupported “250+ cities / almost none reach workers” statistic.** A plausible unmet need is not quantified evidence. A worksite interview and observation of the current heat-alert workflow are more persuasive than an unverified national count.
9. **Satellite land-surface temperature is not worker WBGT.** Treat a city map as optional context; it cannot represent current shaded/radiant exposure and wind at a worker. Remove the ward layer until the core measurements work.

### Buildable MVP and demo

One shared station, two alert badges, local gateway/dashboard, timestamped environmental data, selected task/PPE/acclimatization assumptions, deterministic alert state, acknowledgement and offline data queue. Borrow a reference WBGT instrument for comparisons if possible. If no reference is accessible, display “experimental heat-exposure estimate” and withdraw calibrated/ISO-compliant language.

Use ESP32 for the badges and gateway connection; select either BLE or a simple LoRa star initially. LoRa is a PHY, and mesh needs extra protocol engineering; LoRaWAN is normally star-oriented. [Semtech FAQ](https://www.semtech.com/design-support/faq/faq-lora/P40). Keep messages compact. A language model may draft a supervisor explanation from trusted structured fields but must not change thresholds or issue autonomous work clearance.

Demo sequence: show station readings and quality status; show labeled prerecorded environmental trace crossing the configured policy threshold; both real badges vibrate; Marathi message plays locally; one worker acknowledges; the other is escalated; disconnect internet and repeat. Separately show live station response to a controlled change, with the reference instrument beside it and settling time visible. This proves delivery, offline behavior and measurement limitations honestly.

### Verification gates — proposed targets, not achieved results

- Measurement: collect paired stabilized readings across shade/sun and at least two airflow settings; publish bias, absolute errors, worst error and equilibration time. A proposed hackathon target is MAE ≤1°C against the reference within the tested envelope; passing it does not confer ISO compliance.
- Decision logic: demonstrate boundary values, hysteresis, stale readings, disconnected sensors, missing task data and acknowledgement timeout. Missing/invalid data must show unavailable, never green/safe.
- Delivery: proposed local alert delivery ≤2 seconds after a valid decision in the tested topology; report missed packets and retries. This latency excludes physical sensor settling.
- Usability: test wearer comfort, accidental acknowledgements, vibration audibility/tactility during representative nonhazardous tasks, and Marathi comprehension with five consenting volunteers. A usability test is not a heat-exposure trial.
- Prediction, if retained: compare environmental forecasts with persistence on whole held-out days, not random overlapping windows. Report horizon, MAE and missed threshold crossings.

### Buyers, competition and budget

**Verified prior art:** Kestrel 5400 provides WBGT, alerts and data logging; its direct US list price was $549 when checked, excluding Indian landed costs. [Manufacturer listing](https://kestrelinstruments.com/category-heat-stress/kestrel-5400-heat-stress-tracker). Kenzen markets intelligent wearables and heat/fatigue monitoring to construction, energy, manufacturing, logistics and agriculture; its vendor claims are not independent outcome evidence. [Kenzen](https://kenzen.com/kci-landing-page/).

**Buyer hypothesis:** the first purchaser is a contractor's safety/EHS manager or facility operator who can act on work schedules. Workers use the badge; a municipality is a potential later purchaser, not a committed buyer. Differentiation hypothesis: shared station economics, offline Marathi delivery, transparent assumptions and acknowledgement records. Test whether supervisors are permitted to stop work, who pays, what current instruments exist, and whether workers view monitoring as protection or productivity surveillance.

**Planning estimates in INR, not supplier quotes:** simple BLE alert badge ₹700–1,500; experimental station ₹2,000–5,000; gateway ₹500–1,500 if a laptop/phone is reused; mounts, cables and spare parts ₹1,000–2,000. One station plus two badges is roughly ₹5,000–12,000 with contingency. Reference instrument purchase/rental, a calibrated airflow instrument, custom PCB, labour and environmental certification are excluded. LoRa, each extra sensor, batteries and a wearable enclosure can invalidate the original ₹950–1,200 full-sensing-badge budget. Obtain line-item local quotes before committing.

Revenue should initially be tested as equipment plus setup/support, not savings from supposedly prevented injuries. Adoption needs a policy owner and maintenance/calibration plan; alerts without authority to rest are not sufficient.

## 2. Refined Water Sentinel: Acoustic Leak Triage with Measured-Loss Verification

**Pitch:** Clamp-on sensing identifies persistent leak-like activity on a defined pipe rig or campus segment. A technician receives supporting audio/features and a verification task. Separate flow measurements establish water loss and potential savings. The demo distinguishes detection, metering and repair verification instead of pretending audio alone measures litres.

### Claims requiring correction

1. **NRW is not identical to leakage.** Unbilled authorized consumption and apparent losses are also conceptually different from physical leakage. Rename the MVP around physical leak triage; do not present a universal “Indian cities lose 30–40%” baseline without a named utility audit, year and definition. Campus-access assumptions are more actionable than an unconfirmed PCMC departmental relationship.
2. **Minimum night flow cannot be computed from contact audio.** It requires metered inflow and an estimate of legitimate night demand; pressure is needed to relate night leakage to daily leakage. A World Bank procurement specification explicitly combines flow, pressure, night consumption and baseline calibration. [World Bank leakage-reduction specification](https://ppp.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2024-09/water%20-%20performance%20based%20leakage%20reduction%20and%20management%20services%20Bid%20doc%20sanitized.pdf). Engineering inference: if supply is intermittent or tanks refill at night, “2–4 AM = leaks” is invalid. Either obtain real metered DMA data or remove MNF from the working MVP and label any replay dataset.
3. **Acoustic class confidence is not leak flow rate.** Use a timed collected leak volume or independently calibrated flow meter for the bench experiment. A rig-specific acoustic regression would need pressure, leak geometry and attachment validation before it could estimate volume; it must not be deployed as a universal city leakage model.
4. **Localization formula is incomplete.** For sensor separation `D`, leak distance `x` from sensor A, common wave velocity `c`, and `Δt = tA − tB`, propagation geometry gives `x = (D + cΔt)/2`. The sign changes if delay convention changes. Distance is not simply delay × velocity. This derivation assumes a leak between sensors, a known path, synchronized sampling and an appropriate velocity. Pipe material, geometry, supports and propagation modes matter. Research experiments on plastic pipes show frequency and attenuation dependence; no universal 100 Hz–2 kHz leak band exists. [Original theoretical/experimental research](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-599X/5/2/29).
5. **A few independent scores do not prove segment attribution.** MVP can honestly show “sensor at segment X detected leak-like activity.” Actual segment inference needs multiple known leak locations, background sources and tested attribution logic. Exclude metre-accurate localization.
6. **ESP32-S3 has a continuous SAR ADC acquisition path; I2S is not shorthand for its internal ADC.** The official driver uses high-speed sampling and DMA. [Espressif continuous ADC documentation](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/stable/esp32s3/api-reference/peripherals/adc/adc_continuous.html). Choose a supported ADC1 path with biasing/anti-alias filtering, or an explicit external audio ADC over I2S. With 4 kHz sampling, 2 kHz is the Nyquist boundary, leaving no filter margin; 8 kHz is a more practical starting point for a tested sub-2-kHz band. Investigate low-frequency response on plastic pipe rather than automatically copying speech MFCC settings.
7. **ADXL1002 is not interchangeable within the quoted ₹1,400–1,700 budget.** Analog Devices lists the sensor starting at $40.88 in 1,000-unit quantities; it is analog-output, ±50 g, with an 11 kHz bandwidth. [ADXL1002 product page](https://www.analog.com/en/products/adxl1002.html). A piezo/preamp route is the economical experiment; specify attachment force and repeatability. A high-spec sensor does not eliminate mounting and analog-front-end work.
8. **The TI NPU MCU exists but is a separate design choice.** MSPM0G5187 is listed with 128 KB flash and 32 KB SRAM and an NPU. Model conversion/support and memory fitting need testing; chip price is not node BOM and it does not replace radio, sensor, board and power. [TI product specification](https://www.ti.com/product/MSPM0G5187?jktype=homepageproduct). Stay on one supported platform for the prototype.
9. **Continuous 2–4 AM listening materially affects duty cycle.** Thirty seconds per ten minutes is 5% while active; two continuous hours plus that schedule for the remaining 22 hours is 3.1 hours/day, or about 12.9%, before radio/sleep overhead. Measure energy before sizing solar. Start bench-powered; solar is stretch.
10. **An 80 dB hall test is not field validation.** Engineering inference: airborne chatter is only one interference source. Pump vibration, valve events, normal outlet flow, clamps being moved, different pressure and pipe material can be more decisive. Define sound-measurement method if reporting dB; do not treat one loud-room result as evidence of generalization.
11. **Do not book avoided CO₂ until leakage is reduced.** Use scenario arithmetic `m³ recovered × measured site kWh/m³ × dated grid factor`; report counterfactual assumptions. The draft's energy and carbon constants remain unverified placeholders, and increased available water need not reduce pumping if demand consumes it. Distinguish potential savings, measured repair savings and certified emissions reductions.

### Buildable MVP and demo

One low-pressure recirculating rig, one chosen pipe material, two clamped piezo nodes, one controlled leak outlet, a calibrated collection vessel, pressure measurement if affordable, ESP32-S3, local inference, and a laptop gateway. Start with spectral band-power/persistence rules; compare a small classifier using the same held-out sessions. Avoid cloud dependencies for detection. The dashboard offers leak-like / normal / uncertain / sensor-invalid, supporting evidence, node location and technician-verification state.

Demo: judge opens either a normal outlet or the controlled leak; system must distinguish the two under tested conditions. Collect leakage for a timed interval, show measured rate and explicitly extrapolated litres/day, then close the leak and verify return to baseline. Disconnect internet and show local alert still works. Send compact events over LoRa or use local WiFi initially; do not stream continuous audio over LoRa. A cloud language model can format an evidence-backed draft work order; it cannot invent measurements or automatically order excavations.

### Verification gates — proposed targets, not achieved results

- Acquire independent recording sessions with several pressure/flow states, leak openings and pump conditions. Reattach the sensor between some sessions. Include normal consumer flow, valve operation, taps/knocks and no-water state.
- Split by complete physical session and, where possible, pipe segment. Never distribute adjacent one-second windows from the same recording across training and test sets.
- Report leak-event recall, false alarms per no-leak hour, detection delay, unknown rate and the precise minimum tested leak rate. An illustrative target is ≥90% event recall for explicitly declared leak rates and ≤1 false alarm/hour over several held-out hours. This is a bench target, not a municipal performance guarantee.
- Compare against a no-ML spectral persistence baseline. A classifier that cannot improve the relevant false-alarm/recall tradeoff is optional complexity.
- Verify collected volume over at least three flow levels and report measurement uncertainty. No water-savings claim comes from classifier accuracy.
- Test stale data, lost packets, unclamped sensor and pump-off conditions; no-signal must not be normal/no-leak.

### Buyers, competition and budget

**Verified prior art:** HWM PermaNet TM GPS already combines acoustic detection, time synchronization and a remote portal; its Hydrophone 2 is positioned for difficult plastic and large-diameter mains. [PermaNet TM GPS](https://www.hwmglobal.com/products/permanet-tm-gps/), [Hydrophone 2](https://www.hwmglobal.com/products/hydrophone-2/). Thus “two layers separates us from the field” and generic high novelty are not defensible. Vendor performance is not proof of your design's performance.

**Buyer hypothesis:** campus facilities, a housing society or industrial-estate maintenance contractor is a more accessible first pilot than a municipal DMA. Buyer value is fewer wasted technician visits and quicker confirmed repair, measured against existing inspection costs. PCMC water operations is a possible scale partner, not a named committed customer. Interview the person who approves repairs, a technician who attaches sensors, and the budget owner. Check access to valves, pressure/flow records, current leak-finding process, acceptable false alarms and time to repair.

**Planning estimates in INR, not supplier quotes:** ESP32-S3 ₹500–1,000; piezo and analog front end ₹150–500; radio/antenna ₹500–1,000 if used; power ₹300–700; clamp/enclosure/connectors ₹300–800. Approximate piezo node ₹1,750–4,000; two-node rig, plumbing/pump, metering aids, gateway and spares roughly ₹7,000–16,000. These estimates exclude solar, reference instruments, ADXL1002, engineering labour, field installation and rugged certification. A shared laptop and mains power save risk, not production cost.

Revenue hypothesis: paid diagnosis/pilot plus sensor rental/support or maintained monitoring. Avoid performance-linked savings charges until before/after metering and maintenance responsibilities are agreed. No top-down market-size or willingness-to-pay claim is substantiated by this desk research.

## Practical choice gates

- Choose Water if you can obtain the rig and stable clamp signals immediately, create independent leak/no-leak trials before polishing UI, and tolerate DSP iteration. Failure to separate normal outlet flow from leak flow is a pivot trigger.
- Choose Heat if you can access a reference WBGT device and a supervisor who will explain actual decision rules. If neither exists, scope to an alert-delivery prototype and estimated environment, not an instrument or personalized protection claim.
- With 24–48 hours and no existing hardware/data, neither original multi-node field proposal is realistic in full. Build one sensing path, one local decision and one observable action. Save mesh, satellite maps, prediction, solar and scale claims for validated extensions.
