# INTERVIEW DAY — TCS PUNE, OFFLINE
**Sat 01 Aug 2026 · Gen AI Engineer · sourced via Covenant Consultants**

---

## ⚠️ CONFIRM WITH THE RECRUITER BY WEDNESDAY 29 JULY

Do not walk in on assumptions. Message your Covenant Consultants contact and get these **in writing**, so you have them on your phone:

1. **Exact venue and gate** — TCS has multiple Pune campuses. The two commonly used for interviews are **Sahyadri Park, Gate 1, Hinjewadi Phase 3, Pune 411057** and **Commerzone IT Park, Building 6, Samrat Ashok Path, Yerwada, Pune 411006**. Hinjewadi and Yerwada are ~20 km and, on a bad day, ~90 minutes apart. Getting this wrong ends the day. ([venue references](https://careerforfreshers.com/walk-in-jobs/tcs-walk-in-interview-2025-2nd-feb-2025/))
2. **Reporting time**, and whether it's a slot or a batch.
3. **Your EP / reference / requisition number** — many TCS interview calls carry one, and security and the coordination desk will ask. If Covenant hasn't given you one, ask explicitly.
4. **Name and mobile number of the on-site TCS coordinator**, or the Covenant person who will be reachable that morning.
5. **The exact document list they want** — ask them to send it, then bring that plus everything in the checklist below.
6. **How many rounds and whether they run same-day.** For a lateral role sourced against a specific opening, expect Technical → Managerial → HR compressed into one visit.
7. **Whether laptops are permitted.** Many TCS campuses restrict personal electronics at security; assume you may have to deposit your bag.

## DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Pack this **Thursday night**, not Friday. Use one clean folder, documents in this order, nothing loose.

### Must carry — originals
- [ ] **Government photo ID — Aadhaar AND PAN.** Carry both. Aadhaar for campus security entry, PAN for HR/verification.
- [ ] **Passport**, if you have one (strongest single ID; also relevant if the role has any onsite element).
- [ ] **10th and 12th marksheets and certificates**
- [ ] **B.Tech — all semester marksheets, degree certificate and provisional certificate** (VIT Pune, 2020-2024, 9.3 CGPA)
- [ ] **ElasticRun offer letter, and the letter confirming your internship-to-full-time conversion**
- [ ] **Last 3 months' payslips** — HR will ask when salary comes up
- [ ] **Latest Form 16 / annual CTC or increment letter** — the credible proof of current CTC
- [ ] **ActualOne Protocol documentation**, if you have any (offer letter, experience letter, anything)

### Must carry — copies
- [ ] **6 printed copies of your CV** on decent paper. You may face three separate interviewers plus a coordinator, and offering a fresh copy is a small but real signal. Print Thursday; do not rely on a Friday-night printer.
- [ ] **1 photocopy of every original above** (many organisations retain copies and return originals)
- [ ] **4 passport-size photographs**

### Also bring
- [ ] Blue and black pens (forms sometimes specify)
- [ ] A plain notepad — for your questions and for scratch work if the technical round asks you to sketch
- [ ] Phone fully charged + power bank
- [ ] Water and something to eat. **Waits between rounds are the defining feature of a TCS offline interview day** — plan for 3-5 hours on site, possibly more.
- [ ] Cash — some campuses require visitor formalities and canteens aren't always card-friendly

### Do NOT bring
- Any ElasticRun laptop, device, badge or internal document. Ever. Carrying a current employer's asset into a competitor's campus is an unforced error with real consequences.
- Anything containing ElasticRun proprietary code or client data.

## HOW THE DAY WILL PROBABLY GO

Based on reported TCS lateral hiring patterns ([PrepInsta](https://prepinsta.com/tcs/recruitment-process/), [foundit](https://www.foundit.in/career-advice/here-is-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-tcs-recruitment-process/)):

| Stage | What happens | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Security & reception** | ID check, visitor pass, phone may be tagged or deposited. Arrive with ID in hand, not in your bag. | 15-30 min |
| **Registration desk** | Sign in, submit CV copy, sometimes a manual application form asking for education history, current CTC, expected CTC, notice period. **Know all four exactly — this form travels with you.** | 15-30 min |
| **Wait** | The longest part of the day. | 30 min - 2 hrs |
| **Technical round** | 30-45 min. Panel of one or two. Deep on your projects, then GenAI fundamentals. | 30-45 min |
| **Wait** | Often means you passed. | 30-90 min |
| **Managerial round** | 20-30 min. Scenarios, ownership, stability, client-readiness. Frequently the person who would own you on a project. | 20-30 min |
| **HR round** | 15-20 min. Reason for change, location, notice, CTC, joining timeline. | 15-20 min |
| **Document verification** | May happen here or later. | 15 min |

**Total on-site: budget 4-6 hours. Clear your entire Saturday. Do not schedule anything after.**

**Important:** rounds may collapse — MR and HR are commonly merged into one sitting for laterals. If someone starts asking about notice period and salary inside what you thought was the managerial round, that is normal and usually a good sign.

## THE MORNING

**Leave time:** target arriving **45 minutes early**. Hinjewadi traffic on the Mumbai-Bangalore highway stretch is genuinely unpredictable even on a Saturday. If you arrive an hour early, sit in a café nearby and re-read your opening — do not sit in the waiting area for an hour, it drains you.

**Dress:** formal. Full-sleeve formal shirt, formal trousers, formal shoes. This is TCS and it is offline — do not test the dress code. Iron it Friday night.

**Eat before you leave.** You will be on-site longer than you expect and food access is not guaranteed.

**Read, in the car, in this order:**
1. Your 90-second opening (out loud, at least twice)
2. The four numbers: **601 items · 474+ WMS · 3 domains · 5 personas**
3. Your salary line and your notice-period line
4. Your questions for them

**Do not** try to learn anything new on Saturday morning. Anything not already in your head stays out of it.

## IN THE ROOM

- **Hand over a CV copy unprompted** when you sit down. Small, and it reads as prepared.
- **Sit up, feet flat, hands visible.** Offline interviews are judged on physical presence in a way video ones aren't.
- **Take notes when they talk.** Especially in MR. It signals you treat their words as information rather than as a break before you speak again.
- **When you don't know something, say so in one sentence and immediately say how you'd find out.** Do not bluff on GenAI internals — the person opposite you may build this for a living.
- **Do not fill silence.** After you deliver a Result, stop. Indian interview panels frequently pause to see whether you'll ramble. Let it sit.
- **Never contradict yourself on a number across rounds.** Experience, CTC, notice period, joining date — these get compared between interviewers. Say the same thing three times.
- **Ask for the interviewer's name at the start if it isn't offered**, and use it once. If they offer a card, take it.

## THE END OF EACH ROUND

Close every round the same way:

> "Thank you — is there anything about my background you'd like me to go deeper on before we finish?"

This surfaces an unspoken objection while you can still address it. If the experience-band concern is sitting unsaid in that person's head, this is the question that lets it out — and you have a prepared answer for it.

## AFTERWARDS

- **Message your Covenant Consultants contact the same evening.** Tell them how it went, which rounds you cleared, and confirm your interest. Consultancy recruiters are actively pushing your file inside TCS; keeping them informed is leverage.
- **Write down, that night, every question you were asked.** Memory decays fast and this is the single most valuable artifact for any subsequent round or any other company.
- **Expect turnaround of roughly 1-2 weeks** for a lateral against a live requisition, though TCS is known for uneven response times. Follow up through Covenant, not directly, unless a TCS person gave you their contact and invited it.

## FAILURE MODES — the five things that actually lose this day

1. **Wrong campus.** Confirm the address in writing. Screenshot it. Screenshot the map pin.
2. **Missing a document HR asks for**, most commonly payslips or the degree certificate. Over-pack the folder.
3. **Inconsistent numbers** across technical, MR and HR. Rehearse: experience, current CTC, expected CTC, notice period.
4. **Getting defensive when the experience band comes up.** You have a prepared, confident answer. Deliver it once, well, and move on.
5. **Running out of energy in round three.** HR is last and it's where the offer terms get set. Eat, hydrate, and don't burn yourself out being brilliant in the waiting area.

---

## ONE LAST THING

Everything about this day is designed to test whether you're steady. The technical answers you already have. The single highest-leverage thing you can do on Saturday morning is arrive early, unhurried, and having eaten — because calm is what a 4-10 year band is really a proxy for, and calm is the one thing you can control completely.
