### Security and the gate

- Expect: ID check, visitor register or e-pass, bag scan, visitor badge issued against your ID.
- **Phones are often tagged, camera-sealed, or deposited.** Assume you may lose phone access for hours — so **print the venue address, the EP number and the Covenant contact number on paper**. Do not rely on your phone for anything load-bearing.
- **Laptops are frequently not permitted.** Don't plan to bring one.
- **Never carry an ElasticRun laptop, badge, SIM, or any internal document onto a TCS campus.** Not once, not in the bag, not "just in case."

### The registration desk

You'll sign in, hand over a CV copy, and often fill a **manual TCS application form**: education history with percentages and years, current CTC, expected CTC, notice period, reason for change. **This form travels with you into every round and gets compared against what you say.** Know all four numbers cold and write them the same way you'll say them.

### In the room

- Hand over a CV copy **unprompted** when you sit down.
- Ask the interviewer's name if it isn't offered; use it once.
- **Take notes when they talk** — especially in MR.
- **When you don't know something:** one sentence admitting it, one sentence on how you'd find out. Do not bluff on GenAI internals; the person opposite may build this for a living.
- **Do not fill silence.** After you deliver a result, stop. Panels pause deliberately to see if you'll ramble.
- **Never contradict yourself on a number across rounds.** Years of experience, current CTC, expected CTC, notice period, joining date — these get compared between interviewers. Say the same thing three times.
- Close every round with: *"Thank you — is there anything about my background you'd like me to go deeper on before we finish?"* It surfaces the unspoken objection while you can still answer it.

### In the waiting area

Don't debrief loudly with other candidates. Don't let someone else's "the panel is rejecting everyone" get into your head. Sit, hydrate, re-read your opening once, then stop revising.

2. **Missing a document at verification** — most often payslips, degree certificate, or now **EPFO service history**. Over-pack.
