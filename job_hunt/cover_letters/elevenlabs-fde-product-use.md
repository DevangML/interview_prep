# ElevenLabs — FDE (Software Engineer)

## "Have you used ElevenLabs — even in a personal or side project? What did you build or explore?"

---

### Submit this (216 words)

Yes — on the trial, for a side project generating tracks to publish on SoundCloud.

I worked with Eleven Music through the app rather than the API: prompting for genre and mood, then regenerating individual sections when a track drifted rather than re-rolling the whole thing. Two things stood out to me as an engineer more than as a listener.

First, section-level regeneration changes the workflow rather than just the output. Most generation tools make you discard everything and try again, which makes the tool a slot machine. Being able to keep a track and repair one part makes it something you can actually iterate against — that's the difference between a demo and a tool someone builds on.

Second, and it's why I chose it: the licensing. Publishing to SoundCloud makes the rights question concrete rather than academic, and Eleven Music being trained on licensed material with commercial use included on paid plans is what makes it usable rather than merely impressive. Most alternatives leave you to work that out on your own.

What I haven't done: I stayed inside the trial and never got to the API. The step I'd want next is the REST API in a pipeline — prompt, generate, review, publish — with the review stage scripted instead of me listening to everything by hand.
