# Job Target Specification: AI-Native "Spec-First" SDE

## 1. The Target Role
**Primary Titles:** 
- AI Product Builder / AI Product Engineer (Most common)
- Agentic Systems Engineer 
- Prompt Engineer & AI Automation Specialist
- Forward-Deployed Engineer (AI Focus)
- No-Code / Low-Code AI Developer

**Core Competency:** 
"Spec-First" or Spec-Driven Development (SDD). The engineer's primary output is logic, architecture, and specifications, not syntax. The core skill is "Context Engineering" — structuring requirements so clearly that an AI agent (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot Workspace) can implement them without error.

## 2. Market Data (Late 2025 / Mid 2026)
- **Global Market Size:** ~1.3M to 1.6M open roles for AI Engineers globally.
- **Spec-First Subset:** 30-40% of new openings explicitly target "AI Product Builders" or "Agentic Engineers."
- **Demand/Supply Gap:** 3.2 to 1 (roughly 3 open roles for every qualified candidate).
- **Location:** Highly remote-first. Key hubs include US (Remote), India (Bengaluru & Pune for AI startups), and London.

## 3. Hiring & Assessment Criteria
- **No DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms):** Startups, AI-native agencies, and enterprise innovation wings have dropped LeetCode. DSA is viewed as irrelevant when AI handles implementation details.
- **No Aptitude Tests:** Standard IQ/Aptitude tests are replaced by "Build Challenges" (e.g., "Build a travel itinerary agent"). Assessed on speed and effectiveness of prompting AI to build a working prototype.
- **YOE (Years of Experience):** The "2 YOE" requirement is highly flexible. "Shipped projects" > "years served." A candidate with 2 YOE and deployed functional AI agents is valued higher than a 10 YOE senior developer with no AI fluency.

## 4. Strategic Execution
- **Stop practicing LeetCode.**
- **Build a Portfolio-First identity.**
- **Output:** Document the process (the "spec" written). The spec itself is the resume.
